# Ian Roberts

Designer and builder working on **agent behaviour** — voice interfaces,
conversation design, and the evaluation of systems that act on someone's
behalf.

Previously across conversation design, product and engineering.

## Current work

- **Last Known Design** — a practice and a body of work on how AI agents
  should behave. Six policies, a pattern and anti-pattern library, and a
  set of drills that put a decision in front of you under enough pressure
  that you have to say what you would actually do.
- **Consulting and workshops** — reviews of agent behaviour, and sessions
  for teams building on top of them.
