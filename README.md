# Ian Roberts

Designer and builder working on **agent behaviour** — voice interfaces,
conversation design, and the evaluation of systems that act on someone's
behalf.

Previously across conversation design, product and engineering, which is why
[Conversation Is the Control Layer](https://book.lastknown.design/) insists
that three questions belong to one person even when they belong to three.

## The book

**[Conversation Is the Control Layer](https://book.lastknown.design/)** —
a practitioner's field guide to designing AI agents that listen, wait and act.

> The behaviour of the system is mostly not in the response. It is in the
> timing, the restraint, the permission, the modality choice, the silence, and
> the moment of disclosure.

70,000 words, free, updated monthly, and every claim in it has a source you
can check. [Read it](https://book.lastknown.design/) ·
[source on GitHub](https://github.com/lastknownvoice/conversation-is-the-control-layer)

## Current work

- **Last Known Voice** — the imprint behind this account. The book, and the

- **Agent behaviour design** — six policies, a pattern and anti-pattern
  library, and a set of drills that put a decision in front of you under
  enough pressure that you have to say what you would actually do.
